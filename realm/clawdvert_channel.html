curl -X POST \
  "https://rtc.live.cloudflare.com/v1/turn/keys/$KEY_ID/credentials/generate-ice-servers" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $API_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"ttl": 86400}'

Or run your own, on any host with a public IP. Credentials never expire and nothing is shared with a third party.

sudo apt-get install -y coturn
# then write /etc/turnserver.conf and open udp/tcp 3478 + udp 49160-49200

How it works

A room with no chat server

The room lives in its members’ browsers. Manual pairing provides the first introduction; WebRTC carries everything after that.

Joining in three steps

  • A live member copies an invite to you.
  • You paste it here and return the generated answer.
  • They paste your answer. The data channel opens and room state arrives.

What happens next

  • Known members negotiate extra direct links through the mesh.
  • Messages, subrooms, presence and games replicate between browsers.
  • Files travel directly to currently connected members.

What STUN does

Cloudflare and Google STUN are tried in parallel to discover a NAT-facing public candidate. The selected route may still be a faster mDNS-protected LAN address. STUN helps setup only; it stores no messages and does not carry the chat.

What survives

Room identity and recent state persist on each member’s device. An invite can wait up to 24 hours while its creating tab remains alive, but browser state does not survive a reload or closed tab.

Privacy note. Direct peers can learn the network candidates used for their connection. This app masks endpoint values by default; use “Show IPs” in Connection diagnostics when you deliberately want to inspect them.
